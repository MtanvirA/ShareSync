        Historical Investment Profile Score

        The final score is a weighted historical assessment based on the following components: Return (30%), Risk (20%), Drawdown (20%), Consistency (15%), and Trend (15%).

        • Historical Return:
          ((Ending Price - Starting Price) / Starting Price) × 100
        • CAGR:
          (Ending Price / Starting Price)^(1 / Years) - 1
        • Daily Return:
          (Close Today / Previous Close) - 1
        • Annualized Volatility:
          Daily Standard Deviation × √252
        • Maximum Drawdown:
          (Current Close - Running Peak) / Running Peak
        • Positive Trading Day Percentage:
          Positive Trading Days / Valid Trading Days × 100
        • Moving Averages:
          50-observation SMA, 200-observation SMA
