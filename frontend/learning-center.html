Market Terminology

Essential terms used on the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) and financial markets.

Stock or Share

A unit of ownership in a public company. When you own a share, you own a small slice of the business and have a proportional claim on its profits.
Example: If a company has 1,000,000 total shares and you buy 1,000 shares, you own 0.1% of that business.

Trading Symbol (Ticker)

A short, standard abbreviation used to identify a company on the exchange without writing out its entire corporate name.
Example: GP represents Grameenphone Ltd, and BATBC represents British American Tobacco Bangladesh.

Current Price & Closing Price

Current Price (LTP) is the price of the most recent trade executed during market hours. Closing Price is the official final price agreed at the end of the trading session.
ShareSync Use: Used to calculate your live portfolio market value and today's gain or loss.

Trading Volume

The total number of shares bought and sold during a trading session. High volume means lots of investors are actively trading the stock.
Example: If 20,571 shares of GP changed hands during the session, the volume for GP that day was 20,571.

Market Capitalization

The total market worth of a company, calculated by multiplying its total outstanding shares by its current share price.
Formula: Market Cap = Total Shares × Share Price. Shows whether a firm is large-cap, mid-cap, or small-cap.

DSEX and DSE Indices

A market index acts as a barometer for the overall exchange. DSEX is the broad market benchmark; DS30 tracks the top 30 blue-chip companies; DSES tracks Shariah-compliant equities.
ShareSync Use: Used as the benchmark to evaluate whether your portfolio beat the market.

Dividend

Cash payouts that profitable companies distribute to their shareholders as a reward for investing in the business.
Example: If a company declares a dividend of ৳12.50 per share and you hold 100 shares, you receive ৳1,250 in cash.

Benchmark

A standard market measurement used as a baseline to compare your investment performance against the broader market.
ShareSync Use: ShareSync compares your portfolio returns against DSEX across matching dates to show if you outperformed the market.

Portfolio and Investment Concepts

Key concepts that explain how ShareSync tracks your investments, costs, and profits.

Portfolio Holdings

The number of shares you currently own in each company. It automatically updates whenever you record a BUY or SELL order.
Formula: Holdings = Total Shares Bought − Total Shares Sold.

BUY and SELL Transactions

BUY orders increase your share count and add cash outflow. SELL orders reduce your share count, lock in realized profit or loss, and bring cash inflow.
ShareSync Protection: You cannot sell more shares than you currently own. The system stops overselling automatically.

Average Purchase Price (Cost Basis)

The volume-weighted average price you paid per share across all your purchase orders, giving larger purchases proportionally more weight.
Example: Buying 10 shares at ৳100 and 5 shares at ৳120 yields a true weighted cost of ৳106.67 per share, not ৳110.00.

Current Market Value

What your shares are worth in cash right now based on the latest market price: Quantity × Current Share Price.
Example: Holding 200 shares of a stock trading at ৳150 gives a current market value of ৳30,000.

Realized Profit or Loss

Actual, locked-in profit or loss made from selling shares. It equals your net sale proceeds (after deducting brokerage fees) minus the original cost of those shares.
Status: This money has left the market and is finalized in your pocket (or booked as a loss).

Unrealized Profit or Loss (Paper Gain/Loss)

The paper gain or loss on shares you still own. It equals Market Value − Invested Cost. It rises and falls daily until you sell.
Example: If you invested ৳20,000 and the shares are now worth ৳23,000, your unrealized gain is +৳3,000 (+15.0%).

Total Portfolio Return

The complete financial return of your portfolio. It combines your open paper gains, closed sales profits, and dividend income received.
Formula: Total Return = Unrealized P/L + Realized P/L + Dividends.

Dividend Income

The total cash earnings received from corporate dividends across your open holdings, boosting your overall portfolio gain.
ShareSync Use: Tracked on your Dividends dashboard and factored directly into your total return percentage.

Benchmark Outperformance (Active Return)

The difference between your portfolio return and the DSEX benchmark return over the exact same dates: Portfolio Return − Benchmark Return.
Example: If your portfolio grew +14% while DSEX rose +9%, your outperformance is +5%.

Formulas Used in ShareSync

The exact mathematical calculations implemented in ShareSync, presented in clean, readable equations with worked examples.

1. Weighted-Average Purchase Price Cost Basis
Weighted Average Buy Price = Total Cost of All Purchases Total Shares Purchased = ∑ (Quantity × Purchase Price) ∑ Quantity
What it means: This calculates the true average price paid per share across all your purchase orders, giving larger orders proportionally more weight than small ones.
Worked Example
You buy 100 shares at ৳200.00 (cost = ৳20,000) and later buy 300 shares at ৳240.00 (cost = ৳72,000):
Average Price = (৳20,000 + ৳72,000) ÷ (100 + 300) = ৳92,000 ÷ 400 = ৳230.00 per share
Illustrative example, not a live market quote. (Notice that ৳230.00 reflects volume weighting, whereas a simple average would incorrectly state ৳220.00).
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: ReportService.GetHoldingsReportAsync & TransactionService
MySQL Function: fn_get_weighted_avg_price(p_portfolio_id, p_company_id)
Edge Case: If there are no BUY transactions, the denominator is zero. The code safely returns 0.00.
2. Current Market Value Valuation
Current Market Value = Current Shares Held × Current Share Price × FX Rate
What it means: The current cash value of your open position based on the latest exchange trading price.
Worked Example
You hold 400 shares of GP at a current market price of ৳241.20 per share (FX Rate = 1.0 for BDT):
Market Value = 400 × ৳241.20 = ৳96,480.00
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: ReportService.GetHoldingsReportAsync
MySQL View: vw_portfolio_holdings.current_market_value
Data Table: companies.current_price, transactions, currency_exchange_rates
3. Realized Profit or Loss Closed Trades
Realized P/L = Net Sale Proceeds − Cost of Sold Shares
               = ((Shares Sold × Sale Price) − Broker Fee) − (Shares Sold × Average Purchase Price)
What it means: The actual profit or loss you lock in when selling shares, accounting for the brokerage fee paid on the trade.
Worked Example
You sell 100 shares at ৳350.00 with a broker fee of ৳140.00. Your average purchase price was ৳300.00:
Net Proceeds = (100 × ৳350.00) − ৳140.00 = ৳34,860.00
Cost of Sold Shares = 100 × ৳300.00 = ৳30,000.00
Realized Profit = ৳34,860.00 − ৳30,000.00 = +৳4,860.00
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: TransactionService.CreateTransactionAsync & AnalyticsService
MySQL Function: fn_calculate_realized_pl(p_portfolio_id, p_company_id)
Fee Rule: Transaction fees are deducted directly from sale proceeds to represent the true cash outcome.
4. Unrealized Profit or Loss (Paper Gain/Loss) Open Positions
Unrealized P/L = Current Market Value − Total Invested Cost
Unrealized P/L (%) = Unrealized P/L Total Invested Cost × 100
What it means: The paper profit or loss on the shares you currently hold. It fluctuates with the market and is only realized once you decide to sell.
Worked Example
You hold 300 shares bought at an average price of ৳230.00 (cost = ৳69,000.00). Today's share price is ৳241.20 (market value = ৳72,360.00):
Unrealized Gain = ৳72,360.00 − ৳69,000.00 = +৳3,360.00
Unrealized Gain (%) = (৳3,360.00 ÷ ৳69,000.00) × 100 = +4.87%
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: ReportService.GetHoldingsReportAsync & AnalyticsService
MySQL Function: fn_calculate_unrealized_pl(p_portfolio_id, p_company_id)
Zero Guard: If invested cost is 0, percentage return safely returns 0.00%.
5. Total Portfolio Return Performance
Total Return = Unrealized P/L + Realized P/L + Dividend Income
Total Return (%) = Total Return Total Invested Cost × 100
What it means: The complete measure of how much money your portfolio made across all sources: paper gains, closed trade profits, and cash dividends.
Worked Example
Your portfolio has +৳3,360 in unrealized gains, +৳4,860 in realized profits, and ৳2,500 in dividend income, against ৳69,000 invested:
Total Return = ৳3,360 + ৳4,860 + ৳2,500 = +৳10,720.00
Total Return (%) = (৳10,720 ÷ ৳69,000) × 100 = +15.54%
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: AnalyticsService.GetAnalyticsAsync
Components: Combines active holdings view, completed trade ledger, and corporate dividend table.
6. Daily Return Price Series
Daily Return (%) = ((Today's Price ÷ Yesterday's Price) − 1) × 100
What it means: The percentage change in a stock's closing price from one trading session to the next.
Worked Example
A stock closed at ৳200.00 yesterday and closes at ৳206.00 today:
Daily Return = ((৳206.00 ÷ ৳200.00) − 1) × 100 = (1.03 − 1) × 100 = +3.00%
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: InvestmentIntelligenceService.GetHistoricalInvestmentProfileAsync
Rule: The earliest observation in a series has no prior price, so it produces no daily return.
7. Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) Growth Rate
CAGR (%) = ((Ending Price ÷ Starting Price)(1 ÷ Years) − 1) × 100
What it means: The smoothed annual growth rate that explains how fast an investment grew year-over-year across multiple years.
Worked Example
A stock rose from ৳100.00 to ৳144.00 over exactly 2 years:
CAGR = ((144 ÷ 100)(1 ÷ 2) − 1) × 100 = (√1.44 − 1) × 100 = (1.20 − 1) × 100 = +20.00% per year
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Short-Period Fallback: If total history is under 1.0 year, exponential annualization math distorts reality. ShareSync automatically falls back to simple Period Return with label "Period Return".
8. Annualized Volatility Risk Measure
Annualized Volatility = Daily Standard Deviation × √252
What it means: A standard measure of price fluctuation over a year. 252 is the standard number of exchange trading sessions in a year.
Worked Example
If an equity's daily returns have a sample standard deviation of 1.25%:
Annualized Volatility = 1.25% × √252 = 1.25% × 15.87 = 19.84%
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: InvestmentIntelligenceService
Interpretation: Volatility measures the width of swings. Lower volatility receives a higher risk score in ShareSync's 0–100 intelligence profile.
9. Maximum Peak-to-Trough Drawdown Capital Risk
Maximum Drawdown (%) = Lowest Trough Price − Historical Peak Price Historical Peak Price × 100
What it means: The worst historical percentage drop from a prior peak to the lowest valley, showing how much you could have lost during a severe downturn.
Worked Example
A stock reached an all-time peak of ৳200.00 and later crashed to ৳140.00 before recovering:
Maximum Drawdown = ((৳140.00 − ৳200.00) ÷ ৳200.00) × 100 = (−৳60.00 ÷ ৳200.00) × 100 = −30.00%
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: InvestmentIntelligenceService evaluates running peaks sequentially over chronological trading dates without look-ahead bias.
10. Simple Moving Averages (SMA50 & SMA200) Trend Indicators
SMA 50 = Sum of the Last 50 Closing Prices 50          SMA 200 = Sum of the Last 200 Closing Prices 200
What it means: Rolling average price lines that smooth out day-to-day noise to reveal intermediate (50-day) and long-term (200-day) price trends.
Worked Example
If the last 50 closing prices for GP add up to ৳12,000.00:
SMA 50 = ৳12,000.00 ÷ 50 = ৳240.00
When the current price (e.g. ৳241.20) sits above both SMA50 (৳240) and SMA200 (৳228), ShareSync classifies the trend as a Stronger Uptrend.
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Insufficient History Rule: If a company has fewer than 50 or 200 trading sessions, SMA returns null. ShareSync never fabricates missing historical data.
11. Benchmark Return & Outperformance (Active Return) Market Comparison
Benchmark Return (%) = ((Ending Index ÷ Starting Index) − 1) × 100
Outperformance (%) = Portfolio Return (%) − Benchmark Return (%)
What it means: Shows whether your personal stock picks outperformed the broader Dhaka Stock Exchange index (DSEX) over the exact same period.
Worked Example
Over a 6-month period, your portfolio achieved a +15.20% return, while the DSEX index rose from 5,000 to 5,500 (+10.00%):
Outperformance = +15.20% − (+10.00%) = +5.20% active return
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: BenchmarkService.ComparePortfolioAsync aligns snapshots strictly against overlapping DSE trading dates.
12. Inflation-Adjusted (Real) Return Purchasing Power
Real Return (%) = 1 + (Nominal Return ÷ 100) 1 + (Cumulative Inflation ÷ 100) − 1 × 100
What it means: Your true investment return in terms of purchasing power after stripping out Bangladesh CPI inflation.
Worked Example
Your portfolio made a nominal return of +25.00% across a period with +15.00% cumulative inflation:
Real Return = ((1 + 0.25) ÷ (1 + 0.15) − 1) × 100 = ((1.25 ÷ 1.15) − 1) × 100 = (1.0870 − 1) × 100 = +8.70%
Illustrative example, not a live market quote.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: InflationService.CalculateRealReturn uses official Bangladesh Bank annual rates from inflation_rates. If rates are missing, it safely returns null.
13. Investment Intelligence Score (0–100) Analytical Score
Overall Score = (0.30 × Return) + (0.20 × Risk) + (0.20 × Drawdown) + (0.15 × Consistency) + (0.15 × Trend)
What it means: ShareSync's proprietary 100-point retrospective evaluation assessing a company's historical 5-year growth, volatility, drawdown protection, and price trend.
Worked Example
A company achieves: Return = 85, Risk = 75, Drawdown = 80, Consistency = 70, Trend = 100:
Overall Score = (0.30 × 85) + (0.20 × 75) + (0.20 × 80) + (0.15 × 70) + (0.15 × 100)
= 25.5 + 15.0 + 16.0 + 10.5 + 15.0 = 82 / 100
Academic Disclaimer: This is a retrospective historical analytical assessment, not future price prediction or financial advice.
Technical details & implementation
Backend Service: InvestmentIntelligenceService.GetHistoricalInvestmentProfileAsync
Components Clamped: Each component is normalized and clamped between 0 and 100. Trend categories: Stronger Uptrend (100), Uptrend (75), Mixed (50), Downtrend (25).

How ShareSync Works

How market prices and your personal trade entries transform into portfolio insights in five simple steps.

1

Collect Data

Gathers market prices from exchange feeds, historical trading archives, and your personal BUY/SELL trade entries.

2

Validate Data

Checks that prices are reasonable, verifies trade dates, and filters out erroneous spikes before any math runs.

3

Store Data

Saves structured records safely into MySQL with exact decimal currency precision and ACID reliability guarantees.

4

Calculate Metrics

High-precision .NET 8 services compute your holdings, average cost basis, realized gains, volatility, and benchmark alpha.

5

Show Insights

Presents clear, responsive summaries, interactive charts, and reports across your desktop and mobile devices.
